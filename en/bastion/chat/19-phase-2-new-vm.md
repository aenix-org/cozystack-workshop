## 19. Phase 2. Bringing the machine up in its new home

**Steps 4 and 6.** The image is out. Now we bring a VM up from it, this time in Cozystack,
and nurse it back to life.

Be ready for this: **there will be no networking inside**. This is not a broken testbed and not a
conversion error — it is an unavoidable consequence of the move, and on step 6 we fix it by
hand on purpose, so that you see what it looks like and how long it takes.
